# Methodology (v0.1)

Housing budget = gross monthly household income × user-selected housing share (not a recommended debt-to-income threshold). Rent credit = max(0, housing budget − base rent). Option premium = purchase price × premium percentage. Accumulated deposit = option premium + rent credit × 12 × option years. Mortgage principal = max(0, purchase price − accumulated deposit). Monthly mortgage payment uses standard level-payment amortisation with monthly interest = annual nominal rate / 12 and mortgage months = years × 12. Mortgage-to-income ratio is indicative only.

**Critical omissions:** net vs gross income, taxes, fees, inflation, house price variation, interest-rate reset, maintenance, insurance, vacancy, investment return, custody, developer return, mortgage underwriting, credit history and land/title diligence. No savings returns are assumed. Positive option premiums are assumed fully credited toward purchase only as a scenario assumption, not a legal rule. Rent credits are not assumed refundable if the option lapses; this must be agreed and legally reviewed.

## Model safeguards
Never describe an option as guaranteed ownership. Do not represent rental credits as mortgage collateral without an executed contract and lender agreement. Obtain independent property valuation and a pre-agreed price formula and dispute mechanism. Separate tenant savings from property-owner funds where legally appropriate. Clearly distinguish the pre-purchase option phase from the post-exercise mortgage phase.
