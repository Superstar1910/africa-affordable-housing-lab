# Uganda legal and regulatory diligence checklist (research, not legal advice)

Before any pilot, obtain local counsel's advice on: title verification and registrable interests; tenancy and option-to-purchase enforceability; consumer disclosures; custody of rent credits and treatment on default, expiry or death; valuation standards and independent valuer appointment; mortgage origination and lender licensing; insurance distribution and supervision; data protection and consent; dispute resolution and court jurisdiction; taxation and stamp duty; treatment of savings and investments; land use and construction approvals.

Do not presume one regulator oversees all elements. Map each activity to its relevant authority and verify current statutes and licensing obligations from primary sources before launch. Valuation disputes should have a contractual independent expert determination process, with clear escalation.
